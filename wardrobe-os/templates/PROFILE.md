## Where it comes from (optional)

If you have a professional analysis — a stylist's report, a color draping, a body-shape
consult — note here what it is and where the file lives. Its conclusions go into
**level 2** of `RULINGS.md`, one numbered rule at a time, each citing this source, so
that later you can tell what came from a professional and what came from your own
experience. Those two things carry different weight, and only one of them yields.

⛔ Do not paste a purchased guide, report or its pages into a public repository. Keep it
local, cite it by page.
